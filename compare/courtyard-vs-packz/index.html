Courtyard vs Packz

The two platforms that publish enough for their packs to be compared directly, and the ways in which that comparison is not as simple as the headline numbers suggest.

Packz's published odds imply a better return than Courtyard's, about 100% of the pack price against 95%. But Courtyard commits to an exact figure while the Packz number is an estimate from value ranges, and Courtyard's buyback can be withdrawn while Packz's pays only in site credit.

Why the headline comparison is misleading

One number is a commitment, the other is an inference. Courtyard states an expected value on each pack page. If that figure is wrong, it is wrong in public and against a specific claim. Packz publishes only the bands a card might fall into, so its return has to be estimated, and depending on where cards actually sit within those ranges the true figure could be anywhere from 79% to 121% of the price.

Both figures rest on the platforms' own valuations. Neither number has been checked against what the cards actually sell for. This is the single biggest open question on this site, and it is the next thing we are testing.

Credit is not cash. A 90% buyback paid in withdrawable funds and a 90% buyback paid in packs-only credit are different products. On Packz the credit can only be spent on more packs, so a sell-back is really a re-roll rather than a refund.

Which to pick, on the evidence so far

If you want the most checkable information before you spend, Courtyard is the only one of the two that commits to a number. If you want the best chance of a pull that beats the pack price, Packz's 30.4% is better than most Courtyard packs, though the best Courtyard packs beat it. If you expect to sell cards back for money rather than more packs, Packz's credit-only buyback is a significant disadvantage.

Avoid the $5 and $10 Packz packs specifically. They are the only packs on either platform with a materially worse return than their stablemates, and they are the ones a newcomer is most likely to try first.

Frequently asked

Is Courtyard or Packz better value?

On the published numbers Packz looks better, at about 100% of the pack price against Courtyard's 95%, but the comparison is not like for like. Courtyard publishes an exact expected value and commits to it. The Packz figure is our own estimate, calculated from value ranges and resting on Packz's own card valuations, with a span of roughly 79% to 121% depending on where cards actually sit in each range. Courtyard also lets you withdraw a buyback while Packz pays only in site credit. A committed 95% that converts to cash is arguably worth more than an estimated 100% that does not.

Which platform has better odds of beating the pack price?

Packz, consistently. Every Packz pack gives a 30.4% chance of a card worth at least the pack price, because all its packs use the same odds template. Courtyard's packs range from 12% to 30% depending on which pack you choose, so the best Courtyard packs beat Packz on this measure and the worst are well behind it.
