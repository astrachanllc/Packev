Alternatives to Fanatics Instant Rips

People look past Fanatics Instant Rips because it is the largest brand in the category, uses third-party Card Ladder valuations, and vaults cards free with no time limit, or because they want something it does not do. Here is what each of the credible alternatives trades away.

There is no platform in this category that is better than Fanatics Instant Rips on every axis. Each of these is better on something and worse on something else, and which matters depends on whether you care most about published odds, withdrawable money, or being allowed to keep what you pull.

How to choose between them

  • If you want to know the odds before you buy: Clutch Packs publishes observed results from 158,556 recorded pulls, and Courtyard publishes an exact expected value on every pack. Everyone else is a step down.
  • If you want to convert cards into money: Arena Club, Tilt Rips, Pickem and Packed all pay into accounts you can withdraw from. Packz, Fanatics, Fast Rips and MintPull do not.
  • If you want to keep what you pull: Courtyard, Arena Club, Fanatics and Tilt Rips vault indefinitely. Pickem gives you three hours, Packed one day, Crown Rips and MintPull seven.
