Alternatives to Courtyard

People look past Courtyard because it publishes an exact expected value on every pack, settles in USDC, and vaults cards indefinitely, or because they want something it does not do. Here is what each of the credible alternatives trades away.

There is no platform in this category that is better than Courtyard on every axis. Each of these is better on something and worse on something else, and which matters depends on whether you care most about published odds, withdrawable money, or being allowed to keep what you pull.

Based on published terms and odds collected 2026-09-21.

Clutch Packs

  • Expected value published
  • Odds published

If you came to Courtyard for the published EV, this is the only platform that goes further: 158,556 recorded pulls published on-chain, averaging 1.01× the pack price. It discloses no buyback terms, which is the trade.

Arena Club

  • Odds published
  • Pays cash, not credit

How to choose between them

  • If you want to know the odds before you buy: Clutch Packs publishes observed results from 158,556 recorded pulls, and Courtyard publishes an exact expected value on every pack. Everyone else is a step down.
  • If you want to convert cards into money: Arena Club, Tilt Rips, Pickem and Packed all pay into accounts you can withdraw from. Packz, Fanatics, Fast Rips and MintPull do not.
  • If you want to keep what you pull: Courtyard, Arena Club, Fanatics and Tilt Rips vault indefinitely. Pickem gives you three hours, Packed one day, Crown Rips and MintPull seven.
