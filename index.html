Every card ripping site, one honest number.

Online pack ripping sites publish odds. They rarely publish the number that decides whether a pack is worth buying: how much of your money comes back, on average, per pack. We calculate it from each platform's own disclosures.

Clutch Packs publishes 158,556 real pack openings verified on-chain, and they average 1.01 times the pack price — yet 74% of those pulls came back worth less than the pack cost. Courtyard posts an expected value of 95% of the price on 79 of its 92 packs. Packz's published odds work out to about 100%. Arena Club and Crown Rips publish no values at all.

Odds collected 2026-09-21. These are the platforms' own figures and valuations, not independently verified sale prices.

Transparency ranking

We rank on transparency first, because it is the one thing a buyer can verify before spending anything. A platform that publishes observed results is making the strongest claim available. One that publishes an expected value is making a checkable commitment. One that publishes only ranges, or per-card hit rates with no values, is not.
