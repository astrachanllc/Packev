Which card ripping sites actually pay out money

Four of the eleven platforms we track let you withdraw. The rest pay credit you can only spend on more packs, or do not say. Here is who pays what, by which method, and with which limits.

Four platforms of eleven pay into an account you can withdraw from: Arena Club, Tilt Rips, Packed and Pickem. Four pay credit that cannot be withdrawn at all. Two are unconfirmed, and one — MintPull — pays withdrawable proceeds but caps them at $250 per seven days.

Three things that catch people out

Deposits are often not withdrawable. MintPull, Packed and Pickem all restrict withdrawals to proceeds from selling inventory. Money you put in can only be spent on packs; only money you get back from cards can come out.

The convenient option is usually the expensive one. On Fanatics, the instant offer pays 84.6% in non-withdrawable credit, while declining it and selling the card on the Fanatics Collect marketplace pays real money. The same logic applies anywhere with a credit-only buyback: the cash route exists, it just runs through shipping and selling the card yourself.

Verification is the real delay. Most platforms require a government ID, and some a liveness check, before a first withdrawal. Doing that in advance rather than at the moment you want your money is worth the ten minutes — particularly on Packed and Pickem, where a clock is running on the card itself.

Frequently asked

Which card ripping sites let you withdraw real money?

Four of the eleven we track pay into something you can withdraw from. Arena Club pays 90% to a bank account or debit card through Stripe. Tilt Rips pays 90% in US dollars or several cryptocurrencies. Packed pays 100% to a debit card, bank, PayPal or Venmo. Pickem pays 90% to a bank card, capped at $2,500 per withdrawal. Packz, Fanatics, Fast Rips and MintPull all pay credit that cannot be withdrawn, and Courtyard settles in USDC with a withdrawal path we have not verified.

Which card ripping site pays to PayPal?

Packed is the only platform we track that lists PayPal and Venmo as withdrawal options, alongside debit card and bank transfer, with a minimum around $5 to $10. Tilt Rips pays in US dollars or cryptocurrency. Arena Club and Pickem pay to a bank account or card. No other platform we track publishes PayPal as an option.

Are there withdrawal limits on card ripping sites?

MintPull caps withdrawals at $250 per seven days, which is the lowest by a wide margin — a $2,500 balance takes ten weeks to withdraw. Pickem caps at $2,500 per withdrawal with a $6 minimum. Packed, Arena Club and Tilt Rips publish no cap. Several platforms, including MintPull and Packed, also restrict withdrawals to proceeds from selling cards, meaning deposits cannot be taken back out.

How long do card ripping withdrawals take?

Packed's FAQ says instant withdrawals average about ten minutes, while its terms allow up to two weeks. MintPull states up to one week. Arena Club pays through Stripe on standard processing times. Fanatics offers free ACH or a $50 wire. Most platforms require identity verification before a first withdrawal, which is usually the longest part of the process.
